# Materials for a prescriptive argument

Provisional proposal: Every school district should require a public impact assessment before signing an AI contract.

Known: district procurement authority varies; small districts have limited legal and evaluation staff; public documentation could improve scrutiny; disclosure may expose security-sensitive or proprietary information.

Unknown: administrative cost, enforceable authority in different jurisdictions, minimum viable assessment fields, effects on procurement time, and whether a pilot or contract-value threshold should trigger the requirement.

Constraint: do not claim the proposal improves learning without evidence.
