# The Empty Chair

Last October, during seventh-period study hall at Mesa Vista School, a thirteen-year-old student named Daniel R. told me that the new tutoring system made him feel “stupid in two languages.” His family had recently moved from a named apartment complex, and his mother works at a named local business. I want to use Daniel's story to argue that schools should involve students before adopting tutoring technology. I have not yet asked Daniel or his parent for permission to publish these details.
