# Research and Thesis Development

## Discovery record

Before thesis lock, record:

| Question | State | Evidence needed | Search path | Dispositive? | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Known / unknown / contested |  |  | Would change thesis? |  |

Search across:

- strongest evidence for the provisional answer;
- strongest evidence against it;
- rival explanations or interpretations;
- boundary cases and populations where it fails;
- affected voices, including people who bear costs;
- implementation evidence and failure modes;
- source interests, incentives, and funders;
- historical or jurisdictional differences;
- the fact that would most change the conclusion.

Record whether research confirmed, narrowed, reframed, or killed each candidate thesis.

## Claim-type gates

### Causal

- What mechanism connects cause and effect?
- What plausible alternative explanations exist?
- What is the relevant counterfactual?
- Does the evidence identify causality or only association?
- Which population, setting, and time period bound the claim?
- What evidence would falsify or materially weaken it?

### Prescriptive

- Who must act, what should they do, and do they have authority?
- Through what mechanism should the action produce the result?
- Is it feasible under time, capacity, legal, political, and budget constraints?
- Who pays, who benefits, and who bears risk?
- What trade-offs, implementation failures, and unintended effects matter?
- What is the status quo or best alternative?
- What would count as success, failure, or a reason to stop?

### Evaluative or moral

- Which governing principle or value supports the judgment?
- Is the principle applied consistently to relevant comparison cases?
- What limiting case prevents overreach?
- Which competing value deserves weight?
- Who bears the burden of the preferred judgment?

### Predictive

- What assumptions drive the prediction?
- What time horizon and uncertainty range apply?
- Which leading indicators would support or contradict it?
- Is the prediction falsifiable and stated proportionately?

### Reframing or synthesis

- What default frame is being replaced?
- What new inference or decision follows from the proposed frame?
- What evidence makes the new frame more useful or accurate?
- What does the new frame obscure or leave out?
- Does the reframe illuminate rather than merely rename?

Choose by explanatory and persuasive adequacy, not rhetorical heat. State the selected thesis in plain language and publication-ready language. Record explicit non-goals.

## Argument model

Use Toulmin's components as functions rather than a mandatory paragraph template:

- claim;
- grounds/evidence;
- warrant linking grounds to claim;
- backing when the warrant is contestable;
- qualifier when scope or certainty requires it;
- rebuttal or alternative when it materially tests the argument.
